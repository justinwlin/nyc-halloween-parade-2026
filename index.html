Where to watch: general tips

  • Stand on the east or west side of 6th Ave anywhere between King St and W 15th St.
  • Most crowded: Bleecker St → 14th St. Get there by about 6 PM if you want the curb there.
  • Easier options: the south end between King St and Houston St, or the north end between W 14th and W 15th. The parade starts at 7 PM at the south end, so the front of it reaches the north end later.
  • The parade lasts around 4 hours. Marchers are let in in waves of 1,000–2,000, so you'll still see plenty if you show up at 8 or 9 PM.
  • If you're meeting friends, pick a spot a block or two off 6th Ave. Phones are unreliable in the crowd.
  • Don't drive. Buses on 6th Ave are rerouted from just after 6 PM.
  • Paid VIP and skip-the-line tickets are on halloween-nyc.com. Watching is free.

Want to march?

  • You have to be in costume.
  • Go to 6th Ave & Canal St between 6:30 and 9 PM. Lineup is on 6th Ave south of Spring St, between Spring and Broome.
  • Approach only from the east or south, via Canal, Sullivan or Broome St. If you're coming from the west, enter only from Canal St. Spring, Dominick and Little Broome St are closed.
  • Once you're in, keep heading north. Police will stop anyone walking south.

Subway tips

  • Spring St (C/E): downtown trains skip it from 5 to 10 PM, and the uptown side is exit only. It's the closest station to the lineup, so it's a good place to get off uptown.
  • 14 St/6 Av (F/M/L): the 16th St side is exit only and the 14th St side is open, with barricades.
  • W 4 St–Wash Sq (A/B/C/D/E/F/M): entrances switch between entry-only and exit-only depending on crowds.
  • Weekend A/C reroute (Fri 10 PM to Mon 5 AM): uptown A runs via the F from Jay St to W 4 St. Uptown C runs via the F to 34 St, then the D. Downtown C runs via the D, then the F.
  • Easier stations: the 1 at Houston St, Christopher St or 18 St. The F/M at 23 St. The A/C/E at Canal St.
  • PATH from NJ: take the 33rd St line to 9th, 14th or 23rd St.
  • The L runs on its normal schedule. After 10 PM, downtown A trains run local from 59 St to Canal.

Street closures (NYC DOT, via FOX 5)

  • Lineup area: 6th Ave from Spring to Canal St, Dominick St (Varick to 6th Ave), Sullivan St (Broome to Spring), and Broome and Spring St (Sullivan to Varick).
  • Route: 6th Ave from Spring St to W 18th St.
  • Dispersal: W 16th, 17th and 18th St (5th to 7th Ave), and 5th Ave and 7th Ave (14th to 19th St).
  • Also closed: W 10th St (6th Ave to Greenwich Ave) and Van Dam St.
